## Before adoption

- Identify the actual Licensor and a permission-request contact. Do not infer a
  legal identity from a GitHub account name.
- Confirm the intended scope includes original documentation and interface assets;
  retain explicit exclusions for reference data and source-derived material.
- Confirm the proposed commissioned-creative-work and organization-use boundaries.
- Review the draft for the intended jurisdiction, especially assent, termination,
  liability, applicable law and enforceability. No jurisdiction is selected here.
- Establish contribution terms before accepting outside code: this grant alone
  does not give the maintainer authority to separately license contributors' work.
- The owner has chosen to proceed without commissioned legal review. Record the
  remaining uncertainty; do not describe this text as legally certified.
- After agreement, adopt the operative text in LICENSE, update notices/package
  metadata and review the public release contents.
