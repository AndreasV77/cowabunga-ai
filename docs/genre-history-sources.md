## Relationship requirements

Treat genre history as a sourced network with multiple possible predecessors.
Preserve the difference between:

- subtype/branch of;
- stylistically influenced by;
- fusion drawing on several traditions;
- regional scene or variant;
- emergence/development period;
- contested classification or alternative historical account.

Each future relationship needs source/version, its own supporting passage or
statement references, date/place qualifiers where known, and uncertainty. A genre's
identifier, its label and an asserted origin are different records of information.
Preserve disagreement; do not turn resemblance or a copied claim into another
independent confirmation.

Historical descent does not automatically prescribe prompt vocabulary: inherited
roots do not mean every present-day song must exhibit every ancestor's traits.
Keep descriptive history, current musical characteristics and Suno response
claims separate. Exact release-year precision or one true parent must not be
invented where the source only supports an approximate period or several influences.
